How the ResumeAI Algorithmic Engine Works

4-Stage deterministic pipeline combining Natural Language Processing with classical Computer Science Data Structures & Algorithms.

Detailed Methodology
STAGE 01

Job Spec NLP Analysis

Normalizes text, removes stopwords, and matches keywords against a 70+ technology skill taxonomy.

O(m) Tokenization
STAGE 02

PDF Entity Extraction

Extracts raw streams via pypdf. Regex patterns parse contact info, degree tier, and years of experience.

pypdf + Regex Heuristics
STAGE 03

TF-IDF Vector Matching

Computes term-frequency inverse document frequency vectors and cosine similarity between JD and candidate profiles.

O(V) Cosine Vector Space
STAGE 04

Merge Sort & Heap Ranking

Ranks all candidates with stable divide-and-conquer Merge Sort and extracts top-k with Max-Heap Priority Queue.

O(n log n) Stable Sort
